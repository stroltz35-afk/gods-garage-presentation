08

Max Jukes

Max Jukes lived in a non-Christian environment, was an uncommitted man who married an ungodly woman. Of their 1,026 descendants:

  • 300 died prematurely
  • 310 spent an aggregate of 5,300 years in poorhouses
  • 150 became criminals (7 murdered)
  • Over 100 were drunkards
  • Half the women were immoral

Jonathan Edwards

Jonathan Edwards lived during the Great Awakening, was a godly man who married a godly woman (Sarah). Of their 1,394 descendants:

  • 13 college presidents & over 100 professors
  • 100 lawyers & 30 judges
  • 66 physicians & 80 public officials (3 U.S. Senators, 3 governors)
  • Many ministers, missionaries, and successful authors
